ABOUT FINAL ACT

Final Act is designed to allow you to filter movies by multiple different mood options. The Home page displays a wide range of mood buttons you can choose from. By default, you can select one mood that matches how you're feeling. If you're in a group or individually feeling multiple moods, you can click the "MORE THAN ONE MOOD" button and use the dropdown menu to select how many moods you want to filter by. After selecting your mood(s), hit the "CONFIRM" button in the bottom right of the screen to be redirected to the Filtered Movies page.

There, you will see various movie details, such as a given movie's: poster, title, description, and rating, with buttons to add each respective movie to Save for Later or Watched. Save for Later stores the movie into the Saved for Later page, and Add to Watched stores the movie into the Watched page. These two pages show the same details as the Filter Movies page, with an option to remove an individual or all movies from a given page.

STEP-BY-STEP INSTRUCTIONS

  1. At the Home page, you'll see a variety of mood buttons to choose from.
  2. For a single mood: Simply click on one mood button that matches how you're feeling.
  3. For multiple moods: Click the "MORE THAN ONE MOOD" button, select the number of moods from the dropdown menu, and then click that many mood buttons.
  4. Once you have selected your mood(s), click the "CONFIRM" button in the bottom right of the screen. If you only selected one mood it will automatically display them.
  5. You will then be redirected to the Filtered Movies page (inaccessible via the navigation bar at the top of each page). It will display various movies matching the mood(s) you selected.
  6. If you wish to watch it at a later date, click the "Save for Later" button to add it to your Saved for Later page.
  7. If you have already seen it before, click the "Add to Watched" button to add it to your Watched page.

Note: If you wish to remove any movie(s) from either the Watched or Saved for Later pages, you must click the "Remove" button on that respective movie card. Additionally, if you wish to remove all movies from either page, you may use the "CLEAR ALL?" button on the bottom right of the screen, and then select the "Confirm Deletion" button to remove all movies from that respective page.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q1. How do I access my Watched and Saved for Later lists on a different computer?

A1. You currently cannot, but we anticipate adding the ability to create and store data on a user created account in the near future!

Q2. How can I filter by one mood?

A2. On the Home page, simply click one mood button that matches how you're feeling.

Q3. How do I filter by more than one mood?

A3. On the Home page, click the "MORE THAN ONE MOOD" button. Then select a number from the dropdown menu that appears - this number represents how many moods you want to filter by. For example, if you select "2," you will be able to select two mood buttons. After selecting your mood buttons, click the "CONFIRM" button on the bottom right to see all the movies which match your selected moods.
